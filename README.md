# 📶 SQL Analysis Project (EDA & Advanced Data Analytics)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) and Advanced Data Analysis using SQL Server. Using CSV datasets, it explores data structures, measures, trends, and relationships to uncover meaningful insights into customer behavior, product performance, and sales trends.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Explore and understand the structure and characteristics of the data.
* Analyze key business measures, trends, and performance.
* Identify patterns, relationships, and changes over time.
* Perform customer and product segmentation.
* Generate meaningful insights to support data-driven decision-making.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Microsoft SQL Server** – Data exploration and analysis
* **SQL Server Management Studio (SSMS)** – SQL development and query execution
* **CSV Files** – Data source
* **SQL** – Data analysis, aggregations, window functions, and reporting

## 📂 Dataset

The project uses CSV files containing business data related to customers, products, and sales. These datasets provide the foundation for exploring business performance and generating analytical insights.

## 🔍 Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

The EDA phase focuses on understanding the dataset and identifying important business metrics.

* **Database Exploration:** Explore database objects, tables, and their structures.
* **Dimension Exploration:** Examine categorical dimensions such as customers, products, and categories.
* **Date Exploration:** Analyze date ranges and identify the available time periods.
* **Measures Exploration:** Identify and calculate key business metrics.
* **Magnitude Analysis:** Understand the scale of business measures across different dimensions.
* **Ranking Analysis:** Rank customers, products, and other business entities based on relevant metrics.

## 📈 Advanced Data Analysis

The advanced analysis phase focuses on identifying deeper patterns and generating actionable business insights.

* **Change Over Time Analysis:** Analyze how business metrics change across different time periods.
* **Cumulative Analysis:** Calculate running totals and cumulative business metrics.
* **Performance Analysis:** Evaluate the performance of products and other business entities against relevant benchmarks.
* **Part-to-Whole Analysis:** Determine the contribution of individual categories or segments to overall business performance.
* **Data Segmentation:** Group customers, products, or other entities into meaningful segments.
* **Reporting:** Present analytical findings in a structured format to support business understanding and decision-making.

## 💡 Key Insights

The analysis aims to uncover:

* Customer purchasing patterns and behavior.
* Product performance and sales contribution.
* Sales trends and changes over time.
* The contribution of individual categories to overall sales.
* Differences in performance across customer and product segments.
